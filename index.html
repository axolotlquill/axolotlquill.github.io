Our mission

A larger world, one voice at a time

Paper Lantern Press is a youth-led literary platform dedicated to bringing young voices into the light.

We believe writing from a young age matters—because writing is not simply something to read or publish, but a way of encountering people, places, memories, and perspectives beyond our own. By creating spaces for stories to be shared and carried forward, we seek to widen the worlds we know.

After all, the world is larger when we choose to listen.

What we do

Three branches

Press

We publish writing and creative work from young contributors, on a rolling basis. But we don’t simply accept or reject—every writer is paired with an editor who helps develop their piece. Writers whose work isn’t ready get real feedback, and are always welcome to revise and resubmit.

Conversations

Interviews with writers, artists, historians, community members, and anyone with a perspective worth sharing—the voices and stories that widen how we see the world.

Community

Taking our work beyond the page through collaborative projects with local communities and organizations.

How it works

We read on a rolling basis, so you can submit any time. Here, a “no” is just a draft that isn’t finished yet.

i

Send us your work

Share a piece of writing or creative work. Any subject, any form — what matters is the care behind it.

ii

Work with an editor

You’re paired with an editor who gives real feedback and helps your piece grow toward publication.

iii

Be published

Your developed piece joins the Press. Not ready yet? You’ll get feedback and an open door to resubmit.
