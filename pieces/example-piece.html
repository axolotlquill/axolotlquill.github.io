Seeds in Borrowed Skin

by Astrid Liu · age 16 · June 2026

This is an example piece, here to show you exactly what a published article looks like. To publish a new writer, copy this file, rename it, and replace this text with their work.

Each paragraph goes inside its own paragraph tag. The reading stays comfortable and wide, the way a literary page should feel—generous margins, serif type, nothing shouting for attention. The writing is the point.

A section heading, if the piece needs one

Longer essays can break into sections. Shorter poems or flash pieces can simply be a few paragraphs. The template bends to whatever the writer actually wrote.

When you’re ready, the author box below carries their name and a short bio—so every writer who passes through Paper Lantern Press leaves with their name in the light.

Astrid Liu
Astrid is a writer based in Taipei and the founder of Paper Lantern Press. She has been writing since she was small, and believes the world gets larger every time someone chooses to listen.
